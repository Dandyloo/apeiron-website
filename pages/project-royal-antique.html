Royal Antique
Home

02
Web Design UI / UX E-Commerce Layout Brand Experience

A heritage furniture brand needed a digital home as refined as the pieces inside it. We built a website that makes every visitor feel like they've just stepped into a palace showroom.

Royal Antique Home is a Ghanaian furniture brand specialising in handcrafted pieces inspired by royal and classical design traditions. Their collection ranges from ornate dining sets to statement lounge chairs — each piece carrying a story of craftsmanship and legacy.

When they came to us, their product was extraordinary but their online presence didn't reflect it. They needed a website that could convey prestige, showcase their catalogue beautifully, and open a clear path for custom orders — all in one cohesive experience.

We designed a multi-page site built around a single principle: every scroll should feel like a tour of a private showroom. Rich imagery, generous white space, and a restrained gold-and-dark palette work together to position the brand exactly where it belongs — at the top of the market.

7 Pages Designed
3 Weeks Delivery
1 Brand Voice

The homepage hero sets the tone immediately — a full-bleed antique piece sits against a deep, warm background while the headline, "Furniture Fit For Royalty," lands with quiet authority. No clutter. No compromise. The visual hierarchy pulls the eye down naturally towards the collection.

Navigation was designed to feel premium but functional — a fixed dark bar with clean type, a standout "Get a Quote" CTA, and a mobile drawer that keeps the experience seamless on every device size.

The product section needed to handle a wide range of pieces — from single-seat throne chairs to full dining suites — without ever feeling like a generic catalogue grid. We used oversized product cards with soft shadows, consistent cropping, and a modal quick-view that invites enquiry without breaking the browsing flow.

Each product card carries just enough information: a name, a quiet category tag, and a single action. The restraint is intentional — in a market like antique furniture, mystery sells. The site surfaces desire, then routes it toward a custom-order conversation.

A great website doesn't just display what you sell — it makes people feel the worth of what they're about to own.

Beyond aesthetics, the site was built for performance. Every image is served through Cloudinary with automatic format optimisation and responsive sizing. Pages load fast, even on mobile connections, so no potential customer bounces before the experience lands.

The result is a digital presence that holds its own against international luxury furniture brands — built right here in Ghana, for a brand that deserves to be seen at that level. That's the kind of work we love doing at Apeiron.

This is a general-purpose placeholder policy, not yet tailored to Apeiron's specific data practices. It should be reviewed and refined — ideally with legal input — before being treated as a binding statement of how we handle data.

1. Introduction

Apeiron Digital Marketing ("Apeiron," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit apeirondigitalmarketing.com or otherwise interact with us.

Our website uses third-party services to operate — for example, image hosting (Cloudinary) and form submission handling. These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Nibbles
N Bites

01
Brand Identity Packaging Design Merchandise Social Media Graphics

Nibbles N Bites needed more than a logo — they needed a full brand personality that matched their product: bold, fun, and impossible to ignore. We built them one from scratch.

Nibbles N Bites is a Ghanaian frozen food brand specialising in quick-bite street-food favourites — samosas, spring rolls, and fried snacks. When they came to us, they had great food but no visual identity to match. They needed branding that could live on packaging, merchandise, and social media simultaneously.

We started with their tagline — "Quick bites, big flavors" — and let it drive every design decision. The result is an identity that's energetic and playful: a hand-crafted bubbly wordmark, a punchy yellow-and-dark palette, and a design language that pops equally on a frozen food container and on a lime-green apron.

4 Deliverables
3 Weeks turnaround
2 Colourways

The packaging design for the Frozen Samosa line needed to do a lot of heavy lifting on the shelf. Bright yellow demanded attention while the dark band provided contrast for product information. The wordmark sits confidently in the top left, the product hero shot dominates the right, and the whole label feels like it's mid-motion — food flying, flavour in the air.

For merchandise — staff aprons and branded tees — we adapted the logo across two colourways: the original dark and a vivid lime green that became the brand's signature colour across all touchpoints.

The social media graphics extended the identity into the digital space — same yellow energy, same flying-food aesthetic. Designed to stop the scroll on Instagram and Facebook, each post leads with the product and lets the brand architecture do the rest.

"Great branding doesn't just look good — it makes people hungry."

From packaging to merchandise to social content, the Nibbles N Bites identity is built to travel — into freezer aisles, onto staff uniforms, and into people's feeds. That's the kind of branding we love building.

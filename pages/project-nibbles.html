Nibbles
N Bites

01
Brand Identity Packaging Design Merchandise Social Media Graphics

Nibbles N Bites needed more than a logo — they needed a full brand personality that matched their product: bold, fun, and impossible to ignore. We built them one from scratch.

What We Did

Nibbles N Bites is a Ghanaian frozen food brand specialising in quick-bite street-food favourites — samosas, spring rolls, and fried snacks packed for busy households and street vendors alike. When they came to us, they had a great product with real traction but no visual identity to match the energy of what they were selling. Everything felt generic. We were brought in to change that completely.

We started where all great branding starts — with the product itself. The tagline "Quick bites, big flavors" became the strategic anchor for every design decision that followed. If it wasn't bold, fun, and impossible to ignore, it didn't make the cut. The brief was clear: build a brand that could live simultaneously on a frozen food shelf, a staff apron, and an Instagram feed — and feel completely at home in all three.

The wordmark was crafted to feel hand-made and energetic — bubbly letterforms with a slight bounce that communicates the brand's personality without a single word of copy. We explored over a dozen directions before landing on the final mark, which balances legibility at small sizes (important for packaging labels) with genuine character at large display sizes (crucial for merchandise and signage).

For the colour system, we committed to a punchy yellow-and-dark palette that practically screams off the shelf. Yellow communicates energy, appetite, and warmth — all exactly right for a snack brand. The dark anchors it with enough sophistication to avoid feeling cheap. Secondary to these, a vivid lime green was introduced as the brand's signature accent colour, used across merchandise and certain social executions to give the identity room to breathe and flex.

The packaging design for the Frozen Samosa line was the most technically demanding deliverable. It had to do serious shelf-work: grab attention in a crowded freezer aisle, communicate product type and quantity clearly, maintain legal food labelling requirements, and still feel completely on-brand. The solution leads with a dramatic yellow band, a confident wordmark top-left, and a hero product shot that feels like the food is mid-motion — flying out of the pack toward you.

Merchandise — staff aprons and branded tees — extended the identity into the physical world. We produced both colourways: the original dark with the yellow wordmark, and the signature lime green which quickly became the team's favourite. On a food stall or in a kitchen, these pieces function as both uniform and advertisement. They turn every team member into a walking brand touchpoint.

The social media graphics brought it all home digitally. Designed around the same yellow-energy aesthetic and flying-food visual language, each post template leads with the product and lets the brand architecture carry the rest. The system is flexible enough that new content can be produced quickly without a designer — the rules are clear, the templates are intuitive, and the results are always unmistakably Nibbles N Bites.
